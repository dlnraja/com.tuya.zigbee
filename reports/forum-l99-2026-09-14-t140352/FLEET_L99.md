## #2225 @dlnraja
Hi VicHY — thanks for the detailed report, that helped a lot. I found the cause on the clrdrnya mmWave radars. After an app update, some radar setting DPs (sensitivity, range, delay) were being restored as curtain/blind capabilities from stored data. That’s the “blind mode” UI, and one of those DPs was also feeding motion, which explains the stuck presence. Delete + re-pair works until the next update because it clears that stored state temporarily. Fix is in Test 9.0.790+: phantom caps are stripped automatically and DynCap no longer re-applies them on update. Please update, restart the app, and check both sensors. Re-pair only if they still show as blinds. The “flooding the Zigbee network” timeline message is the app rate-limiting ~200 msg/min from the Tuya firmware — related to the stuck presence spam, and it should calm down once the sensor is healed. Let me know if it persists after
